# Getting Started

Welcome to StreamyFin! This guide will help you get up and running quickly.

## What is StreamyFin?

StreamyFin is a native iOS and Android client for Jellyfin media server, designed to provide a seamless streaming experience across all your devices.

## Quick Start

1. **Install StreamyFin** from the App Store or Google Play
2. **Connect to your Jellyfin server** using your server address
3. **Sign in** with your Jellyfin credentials
4. **Start streaming** your media content
